The stay

A haveli reborn

Set in Civil Lines, The Peacock pairs restored jharokha windows and a shaded courtyard with quiet, modern rooms. Mornings start on the rooftop with chai and the Aravalli skyline; evenings end by the pool.

Twelve rooms, two dining spaces, and a team that remembers your name — close to the City Palace and Hawa Mahal, far from the noise.
